# Amazon Customer Support Knowledge Base: Pricing & Promotions

## 1. Scope & Associated Intents
- `pricing_or_promotion`: Questions regarding price adjustments, price matching policy, post-purchase price drops, coupon and promo code application, promotional credits, Lightning Deals, Prime Day/Black Friday discounts, bundle pricing, and currency conversion fees.

---

## 2. Core Policies & Promotional Rules

### Price Matching & Post-Purchase Price Drops
- **General Policy**: Amazon works constantly to offer low prices, but **does not offer post-purchase price matching** or partial refunds if an item's price drops after the order has been placed or dispatched.
- **Customer Option**: If an item's price drops significantly, customers may choose to return the original item within the standard 30-day return window (if eligible) and place a new order at the lower price.
- **Pre-Order Price Guarantee**: For physical items ordered prior to official release, customers are automatically charged the lowest price offered on Amazon between the time of order and the release/dispatch date.

### Coupons & Promotional Codes
- **Application**: Coupons must be "clipped" on the product detail page before adding the item to the cart. Promotional codes must be entered on the final checkout review screen.
- **Restrictions**:
  - Most promotional codes apply strictly to items **Sold and Shipped by Amazon** (Retail), not third-party marketplace sellers.
  - Promotional codes typically cannot be combined with other offers or applied retroactively to completed orders.
  - Promotional codes expire on the designated date and have no cash value.

### Lightning Deals & Timed Sales
- Lightning Deals have limited inventory and time limits.
- When claimed, a customer has **15 minutes** to complete the purchase before the deal is forfeited to the next customer on the waitlist.

## 3. Communication Guidelines & Tone on Twitter/Social Media
- **Tone**: Transparent, courteous, firm yet empathetic.
- **Honest Policy Explanations**: Do not make false promises regarding price adjustments. Clearly explain the price matching policy politely and suggest returning/re-ordering if economical.
- **Never Solicit Promo Codes**: Never distribute internal concession codes or promised credits over public social channels.

---

## 4. Decision Matrix: Auto-Handle vs. Human Escalation

| Scenario | Recommendation | Reason / Escalation Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Customer asks for a partial refund because price dropped after delivery | **Auto-Handle** | Policy explanation; politely explain Amazon does not price match post-purchase, note 30-day return option. |
| Customer asks how to apply a coupon or promotional code at checkout | **Auto-Handle** | Navigation guidance; explain clipping coupons on product pages and entering code at checkout review screen. |
| Customer missed a Lightning Deal window and asks to buy at deal price | **Auto-Handle** | Informational; explain Lightning Deals are limited time/quantity and encourage watching for future deals. |
| Prior customer service rep promised a promotional credit in chat/email, but it was not applied | **Escalate to Human** | CS commitment verification; requires checking internal contacts/memos and manually issuing concession credit. |
| Promotional credit was deducted for an order that was subsequently cancelled by Amazon, but credit was not refunded to balance | **Escalate to Human** | System balance glitch requiring manual replenishment of promotional gift balance. |
| Customer charged an unexpected currency conversion fee or incorrect exchange rate applied | **Escalate to Human** | Billing investigation; requires reviewing Amazon Currency Converter (ACC) transaction records. |

## 5. Canonical Response Templates

### Template: Price Drop / Price Match Explanation (Policy Standard)
> "Thanks for contacting us! We constantly update our prices to offer the best value, but we don't offer post-order price matching or partial adjustments if a price drops after purchase. If your order is within our 30-day return window, you are welcome to return the item and place a new order at the current price! ^CS"

### Template: Applying Promo Codes (Self-Serve)
> "To apply a promotional code, enter the code into the 'Gift Cards, Vouchers & Promotional Codes' box on the final checkout screen before placing your order. Make sure the item is sold directly by Amazon, as many promo codes don't apply to third-party seller items. Let us know if you run into any issues! ^CS"

### Template: Missing Promotional Credit Escalation (Human Escalation)
> "We want to make sure any promised credits or promotional discounts are properly honored on your account. Because we'll need to check your order history securely, please send our team a DM here: [DM_LINK] so we can get this sorted out for you! ^CS"
